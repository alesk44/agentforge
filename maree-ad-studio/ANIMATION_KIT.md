# Marée Jersey — reusable cinematic animation kit

## Direction
Premium cinematic motion design, not live-action footage and not photorealistic AI video. Original vector/painted graphic scenes with layered depth, parallax, animated sea, camera drift, atmospheric grain, restrained navy/teal/coral palette and documentary pacing.

## Zero-cost production boundary
- Python + Pillow + FFmpeg + eSpeak NG; no paid API, stock subscription, GPU or new recurring service.
- GitHub Actions uses the standard hosted runner. Public repositories currently qualify for standard runner usage under GitHub's published plan limits; check account limits before repeated renders.
- The animation renderer creates original graphic scenes. It does not create photorealistic humans, documentary footage or actual vessel footage.
- No third-party footage or location imagery is required for this animated edition.
- Optional music/SFX must be original or carry an explicit commercial-use licence. The default render must work without optional music.

## Visual system
- Deep Atlantic navy, blue-green sea, pale foam, warm dawn highlights, coral interface accents.
- Cinematic composition: broad negative space, foreground/midground/background layers, slow camera drift, restrained transitions.
- Jersey identity: stylised coastline, tidal flats, harbour walls and a recognisable Mont-Orgueil-inspired castle silhouette. It is an original illustration, not a claim to depict a precise real camera view.
- Boat, fisherman, chef, fish, delivery vehicle and phones are original stylised illustrations.
- Official Marée Jersey logo is an immutable supplied asset. Only uniform scaling is allowed; never redraw, recolour, crop, distort or add effects to it.

## Scene kit
1. Island dawn: layered sea, Jersey coastline silhouette, sunrise and fishing boat.
2. Catch capture: working deck, fisherman silhouette, one fish and a phone camera frame.
3. Catch identity: same fish motif, species and traceability information in a clearly labelled demo app card.
4. Restaurant discovery: chef silhouette, tablet and the same catch card.
5. Order: order confirmation card and fisherman notification.
6. Handover: insulated crate, stylised van route and chef receiving the same fish; under-one-hour remains an illustrative campaign scenario only.
7. Brand end-card: clean navy/sea texture, exact official logo asset, campaign line.

## Reuse
- Keep scene artwork modular: sea, coastline, boat, fish, characters, phone/tablet cards, crate, van and end-card.
- Use the same fish illustration and demo species label throughout all scenes.
- Reuse scene assets in 9:16 and 16:9 crops; keep important text and logo within safe margins.
- Reuse the kit for 6–15 second social cutdowns, website hero loops and launch posts.

## Truth and release gates
- UI is marked DEMO wherever it implies unverified app functionality.
- No real fisherman, vessel, restaurant, catch date, fishing method, price, QR record, certification or delivery performance is invented.
- “UNDER 1 HOUR” must be accompanied by “ILLUSTRATIVE CAMPAIGN SCENARIO” until operational evidence supports a service promise.
- Never publish the campaign as a guarantee of cold-chain compliance or delivery time without documented operational validation.
- Official logo must be checked against the supplied source image before release.
