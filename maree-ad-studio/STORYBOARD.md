# Marée Jersey — Film 01: From Boat to Plate

**Production format:** 45-second original cinematic 2.5D animation.  
**Primary master:** 9:16 (1080×1920); adapt the same sequence to 16:9 (1920×1080).  
**Master line:** MARÉE JERSEY — FROM BOAT TO PLATE.  
**Supporting line:** One island. One sea. One connection.

## Creative direction
Premium animated documentary mood: Atlantic navy and teal, dawn warmth, restrained coral app accents, layered sea and coastline, subtle parallax, slow camera drift, animated waves and clean interface inserts. Original stylised Jersey geography evokes the island without pretending to be a real aerial shot. No third-party footage, stock imagery or location filming is required for this edition.
