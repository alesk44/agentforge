# Marée Jersey — Film 01: From Boat to Plate

**Production status:** storyboard / production specification. Not a claim that the depicted app features or delivery SLA are already live.

## Creative objective
Tell one continuous, believable Jersey seafood transaction: a local fisherman catches a fish, records it in Marée Jersey, a restaurant discovers it, orders it, and receives it for service. The island must be recognisable from the geography and working waterfront, not just from a title card.

**Master line:** MARÉE JERSEY — FROM BOAT TO PLATE.
**Supporting line:** One island. One sea. One connection.
**Primary master:** 45 seconds, 9:16 (1080×1920). Adapt a 16:9 cut from the same timeline.
**Look:** premium documentary realism, natural sea light, handheld details, restrained teal/navy/coral graphic overlays, quick but legible app inserts. Avoid generic stock montage, fake UI clutter, overdone CGI and tourist-ad clichés.

## Jersey visual bible
Use real, geographically correct references:
- St Helier Harbour / La Collette / Elizabeth Castle for harbour and island context.
- Gorey Harbour with Mont Orgueil as a strong east-coast establishing shot.
- Rozel Harbour for a smaller traditional fishing-harbour feel.
- St Aubin's Bay / Elizabeth Castle for tidal geography and aerial transitions.
- Jersey's rocky reefs, broad intertidal shore, changing tide and Channel light.
Do not put a landmark in the wrong harbour or imply a generic fishing vessel is a verified Jersey vessel. Prefer actual local working-boat footage or clearly neutral close-ups where vessel identity is not established.

## Timed sequence and shot direction

### 00:00–00:06 — 1. Jersey wakes up
**Picture:** dawn aerial / sea-level glide over a recognisable Jersey coast; cut to a real local fishing boat leaving or working offshore. Keep coastline and tide believable.
**Sound:** wind, gulls, engine, water. Music begins subtly.
**On-screen:** JERSEY. ONE ISLAND. ONE SEA.
**Voice-over:** “Around Jersey, every catch begins with the sea.”

### 00:06–00:13 — 2. Catch and capture
**Picture:** fisherman brings in a fish; respectful, authentic handling on deck. Close-up of the fish, then phone camera photographing it. The phone screen clearly shows a capture action.
**App insert (demo UI):** NEW CATCH → photo thumbnail → IDENTIFY CATCH.
**Voice-over:** “A local fisherman lands his catch — and records it in seconds.”

### 00:13–00:20 — 3. Identity and traceability
**Picture:** close-up of phone in fisherman's hand; keep real fish visible beside it. Clean, premium app UI.
**App insert (demo UI):** SPECIES: [CONFIRMED SPECIES]; CAUGHT: [DATE & TIME]; VESSEL: [VERIFIED VESSEL NAME]; METHOD: [DECLARED METHOD]; ORIGIN: JERSEY WATERS; TRACEABILITY DETAILS.
Use clearly marked fictional/demo data during production until genuine records are supplied. Never invent a vessel, catch time, fishing method, QR record or verified status.
**Voice-over:** “Its species, origin, catch details and fishing method are brought together, so the restaurant can see where its seafood comes from.”

### 00:20–00:28 — 4. The restaurant discovers the catch
**Picture:** chef in a real Jersey restaurant kitchen checks a tablet/phone. Match cut from fish photo on fisherman's phone to the same fish listing on chef's screen.
**App insert (demo UI):** JUST LANDED / same fish image / species / method / origin / availability / indicative price only if supplied / RECIPE IDEA.
Recipe suggestion must match the confirmed species and be presented as inspiration, not a guaranteed dish.
**Voice-over:** “In the kitchen, that very catch appears — with its story and an idea for the menu.”

### 00:28–00:34 — 5. Order placed
**Picture:** chef taps PLACE ORDER; clear confirmation on screen. Cut to fisherman receiving the order notification and preparing the fish for dispatch.
**App insert (demo UI):** ORDER CONFIRMED with order reference marked DEMO.
**Voice-over:** “The chef places an order directly. The fisherman gets the confirmation.”

### 00:34–00:40 — 6. From boat to kitchen
**Picture:** fast, grounded delivery montage: packed fresh fish, vehicle leaving harbour area, restaurant handover, chef receives it. Show a UNDER 1 HOUR graphic only as a clearly illustrative campaign scenario unless the service-level promise has been validated operationally.
**Voice-over:** “From the coast to the kitchen — in our launch story, delivered in under an hour.”
Do not imply cold-chain compliance or actual delivery performance without evidence.

### 00:40–00:45 — 7. Brand reveal
**Picture:** confident close-up of the Marée Jersey app on a phone; transition to the exact official logo asset, unchanged, on a clean navy/sea-texture end card.
**On-screen:** MARÉE JERSEY / FROM BOAT TO PLATE. / ONE ISLAND. ONE SEA. ONE CONNECTION. / DISCOVER THE PLATFORM / mareejersey@gmail.com
**Voice-over:** “Marée Jersey. From boat to plate.”
Hold the final logo cleanly for readability. Never redraw, recolour or replace the supplied official logo.

## Production truth / safeguards
- App screens are illustrative until each feature is verified in the live Marée Jersey build.
- Use a single confirmed fish species throughout all shots; do not substitute a different fish between catch, app listing, chef screen and delivery.
- No invented fisherman identity, vessel name/registration, catch metadata, QR traceability, restaurant partner, price or delivery proof.
- The under-one-hour delivery is a scenario, not a service guarantee, until operations can support it.
- The supplied official logo must be used as-is. The current repository does not yet contain a clean standalone logo asset.
- No paid API, paid stock subscription, paid GPU or new recurring cost without explicit approval.
