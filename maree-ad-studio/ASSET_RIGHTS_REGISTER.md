## Key rights finding
Visit Jersey's Media Asset Library says downloaded assets are to be used solely for promoting Jersey as a tourist destination, in line with the individual usage guidelines; other uses are not permitted without clarification. Marée Jersey's commercial marketplace advert is not automatically covered. Ask Visit Jersey for written permission before using any of its footage.

Jersey Heritage says commercial or promotional filming at its visitor sites requires advance approval. This is relevant if we plan to film at Mont Orgueil or Elizabeth Castle visitor sites.

The Jersey Evening Post Photo Store offers commercial licences for some images, but its listed social-media image tier explicitly excludes advertising. Confirm the exact licence needed for paid or organic commercial promotion before purchasing.
